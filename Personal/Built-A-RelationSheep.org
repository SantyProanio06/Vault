The person you choose to share your life whit, is the most important decision in your life, no matter what happened to you, you will always have someone who will support you, a shoulder to rest. Even entrepreneurs when fail in biz have someone who loves them.  
** Sexuality
According to Pep, the body is needed to express love, huggin', kissin', etc, some who can't move, can't  express love even if he's alive.

There's levels of intimacy in relations, like givin' the hand to someone, smiling at 'em, etc, sex is the maximum level of intimacy between two bodies, and it's reserved to only 1 person.

Sexuality is made to union two people, sons are the product of the love of a relationship.

Oxytocin binds: in a sexual relationship, oxytocin is released, and its the connection hormone.

We should know someone really deep, 'cause this is to this person who i am gonna give all my soul all my being, that's sex, givin' someone your all, that's why the church gives this advice of waitin' 'till marriage that's a good and healthy sexuality. Give 'n receive.

Usually in TV shows, first there's the sexual relationship and then is when they know each other, the order of the factors change the product, what we should look for is knowing the other person, and then doing the act.

You don't decide when you fall in love, you just fall in love. But you have to pass all those feelings through the head, to decide if this is good or bad for you, this moment is being called lucid moment. First feelin' then reason.

Is really important the non verbal language, you have to look a lot, and know the other talkin' a lot too.

The biggest red flag is acts of disrespect, do not tolerate 'em. the same is with abuses.

* How to build an strong relation
Putt effort on it, don't wait 'till feelin' o stop feelin' somethin', be aware of each other, speak 'bout everything, talk 'bout how things you want it to go.

Love the other one, no matter what, every day of the life until i die. Marriage is not somethin' to take softly, is somethin' you have to take with all the seriously possible.

When you work your love, the feelin' comes back, this is the difference between bear and love each other. For example, the next morning i wake up and go for the cake she loves in the bakery she loves. If i wait 'till the feeling you can wait forever.

Is normal to not feel the same you felt in the beginning but you have to put effort in your love. You have to educate your children to leave home. You can't disregard your partner during that stage where rising your children demands you a lot, YOU CAN'T. So when you come back, you forget each other and don't take care of each other(ex. Stop training 'cause of the warm that love brings). 

Communication is on the most important things, you have to tell the good and the bad things, with affection obviously, but also you have to ask also about what the other don't like 'bout you, and things like that, the thing is doing 'em at any time, not only when you fight, do not stay in superficiality.

The thing is, talkin' about this things when you are in the best of the relationship, (what is the thing about me that you would like me to change, how do you feel, etc) you have to work on this things.

"The most important thing is my wife".

Love is not only a feelin' you have to have the volition to do it. Love is a decision.

"Happy is the man than marries the woman he loves, but is happier the man that loves the woman he married." We marry to love each other, not only love to get married.

Do not thing on big things (a trip, for ex) in one day, no, thing 'bout small things, a wine, go for a walk, etc. but every day.

You have to give all of you to the person you marry. Marriage is way to radical.

In everything put god in the center, not only in love. If you were certain, you wouldn't have faith.
