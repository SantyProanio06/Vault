* How to build an strong relation
Putt effort on it, don't wait 'till feelin' o stop feelin' somethin', be aware of each other, speak 'bout everything, talk 'bout how things you want it to go.

Love the other one, no matter what, every day of the life until i die. Marriage is not somethin' to take softly, is somethin' you have to take with all the seriously possible.

When you work your love, the feelin' comes back, this is the difference between bear and love each other. For example, the next morning i wake up and go for the cake she loves in the bakery she loves. If i wait 'till the feeling you can wait forever.

Is normal to not feel the same you felt in the beginning but you have to put effort in your love. You have to educate your children to leave home. You can't disregard your partner during that stage where rising your children demands you a lot, YOU CAN'T. So when you come back, you forget each other and don't take care of each other(ex. Stop training 'cause of the warm that love brings). 

Communication is on the most important things, you have to tell the good and the bad things, with affection obviously, but also you have to ask also about what the other don't like 'bout you, and things like that, the thing is doing 'em at any time, not only when you fight, do not stay in superficiality.

The thing is, talkin' about this things when you are in the best of the relationship, (what is the thing about me that you would like me to change, how do you feel, etc) you have to work on this things.

"The most important thing is my wife".

Love is not only a feelin' you have to have the volition to do it. Love is a decision.

"Happy is the man than marries the woman he loves, but is happier the man that loves the woman he married." We marry to love each other, not only love to get married.

Do not thing on big things (a trip, for ex) in one day, no, thing 'bout small things, a wine, go for a walk, etc. but every day.

You have to give all of you to the person you marry. Marriage is way to radical.

In everything put god in the center, not only in love. If you were certain, you wouldn't have faith.

If things goes wrong, do not speak bad 'bout your ex-partner, "we suffer more about what we say than the reason of why we break up".

The most important thing in a marriage is the small things!

** The hug technique
Give a long hug to your partner in the morning and in the night mandatorily, and if you haver children they must see you, 'cause 1 hug with your partner in front of your children is worth 100 to every one of 'em. The thing is to show 'em that you love each other.

** SOS
If your marriage is having troubles apply the SOS technique, Silence Oration 'n' Service.
- Silence: Do not argue, you know you are having troubles, so if you argue is gonna be worse.
- Oration: Pray a lot, the one for the other.
- Service: Do things for the other, let me do this, let me handle this, etc. Think more in the other than in yourself.
  
But if things didn't get fixed, go to a therapist, but one with good criticism, before things goes bigger and harder to handle.

** Good advice to be good parents
I know i'm not that religious, but he says you have to live like a good christian, to inspire and give people confidence in your religion, that's the way you expand your religion without being like conquers. What you have to do is living your religion.

Delegate things. One of the children is delegated to do somethin' for example, the one who is delegated with shoes has to clean the shoes of all of the members of the family.

Put god in the center. But to rise good children there's 4 blocks of values 'n' virtues:
1. Spirituality a.k.a God
2. Family 
3. Work
4. Friends, habits etc.

With your partner you have to ask each other what is the most important thing? (choosing in this 4 blocks), and then to your children, between 7 or 10 years old, ask What do you think is the most important thing to me?. And you listen, the objective to this question is to know if your children are recepting the most important thing you are trying to transmit. If you like their answer you say yes that's it, if you don't like it you have to tell 'em what really is and change the way you transmit. Because usually the children say work or money, 'cause one is the thing you are trying to transmit and other is the thing your children recept. We give example every time, not only when you try to, every time.
